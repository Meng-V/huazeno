Hebei Huazeno Plastic Packaging Co., Ltd. was founded in 2002 with registered capital of RMB 50 million on a 240-mu site, and today has 110,000 square metres of built floor area. The company manufactures plastic woven bags and related products. It employs over 700 people, including 60 managers and technicians holding college or vocational degrees, and operates 1,300 sets of plastic-weaving equipment. Annual production and sales exceed 500 million woven units. Annual profit and tax exceed RMB 7 million. Over eighteen years, Huazeno has grown from a small woven-bag workshop into one of the larger plastic-weaving manufacturers in China.
